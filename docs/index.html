What it does

A small app with a clear job: make herbal knowledge easy to browse and easy to put into practice.

🔎

Search and filter

Find herbs by name or by the symptom you want to address, then narrow the list with filters.

🌿

Detailed herb pages

Scientific name, family, climate, soil, sunlight, active compounds, therapeutic properties and dosage forms for each herb.

🪴

My Garden

Add herbs you grow, see care tasks for today, and follow each plant's growth stage from seed to mature plant.

🔔

Alerts and seasonal tips

Season-aware advice and quick care tips on the home screen, plus an alerts screen for reminders.

🧠

Ayurvedic quiz

An eight-question quiz about classic Ayurvedic herbs with instant feedback and a running score.

👤

Accounts and profile

Sign up, log in, edit your profile and replay the tutorial. State is saved on the device.
